Polygon Feature - 1-Day Sprint Plan (MVP)

All 8 days of Drone_Change_Detection_Task_Breakdown.docx compressed to ONE day  |  Uday (GPU) + Priyanka (CPU) in parallel  |  01 Aug 2026

Scope rule for a 1-day build

Ship the MINIMUM polygon pipeline end-to-end: engine extraction -> API serialization -> viewer overlay -> GeoJSON export, with bbox fallback everywhere. Everything cosmetic or defensive is cut (list at the end). If any step overruns its timebox, cut its remaining polish and move on - the end-to-end chain is the deliverable.

Reminder: polygons improve report readability, not F1. Keep the accuracy loop (verified-pair labeling + retrain + frozen-test eval) running as agreed separately - this sprint must not consume more than the one day.

First 15 minutes (both, together)

Contract: Agree the region-dict contract: polygon = [[x,y], ...] image-px closed ring, max 60 vertices; absent key = no polygon (bbox fallback). NO polygon_holes in the 1-day MVP.

Agree the merge point: Uday pushes extraction by midday; Priyanka stubs the field until then and swaps to real output after lunch.
